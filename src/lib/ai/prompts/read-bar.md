# Bar menus

- Bar menus mix cocktails, beer, wine, spirits, and bar food. Decide food or beverage item by item.
- In a spirits list, each bottling is an item. Keep a pour size printed in the heading with the section name: "Scotch (2 oz)".
- Beer: the name and style as printed. The brewery and its city are terms.
- Days or hours of availability, such as a happy hour window, go in a "Policy:" reader note.
- Rotating offers, such as a "pick of the week" or a "classic pour", are house terms to confirm: ask what they currently include.
