# Food menus

- Courses and categories, such as raw bar, appetizers, mains, sides, and desserts, are sections.
- Keep portion sizes and cuts in the name as printed: "8oz Center-Cut Filet Mignon".
- "MP" or "Market Price" is the printed price, and amount is null.
- A tasting or prix fixe menu is a section named for that menu, with its shared price kept in the section name. Each course is an item.
- Sauces, butters, and accompaniments offered with entrées are items in their own section.
- Dessert menus often carry after-dinner drinks. Those items are beverages.
- Food menus carry most allergens and most raw or undercooked warnings. Apply those rules with the most care here.
