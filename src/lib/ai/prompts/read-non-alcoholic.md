# Non-alcoholic menus

- Zero-proof cocktails, sodas, juices, coffee, tea, and waters are all beverage items.
- Terms: non-alcoholic spirit brands, tea types and origins, coffee preparations, and house syrups.
- Never call a drink alcohol-free unless the menu says so.
- Allergens: record only what the menu names, such as milk or cream (milk). A latte or cappuccino alone records nothing, since many bars offer other milks.
