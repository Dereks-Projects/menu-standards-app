# Specials

- Specials are often handwritten boards or quick printouts. Read slowly. Give any word you are unsure of a lower confidence and an "Uncertain:" reader note.
- Never complete a word you cannot read. Copy what is legible.
- A date or day printed with the specials goes in a "Policy:" reader note.
- "MP" or "Market Price" is the printed price, and amount is null.
